## Goal

100x the Archive layout so it reads Apple-caliber, not slop: make the TASTING the hero of every entry (★ rating + one-word + a pull-quote from the note), keep Tal's structure (favourite cups highlighted on top, the rest chronological), and add iOS-native motion (scroll-reveal stagger, parallax bag thumbnails, spring + haptics, pinning year headers) on top of the existing hero morph. Declutter the redundant "Show Details" expander and row flavor-tag chips, and surface the full tasting (stars + aroma/body/finish + notes) on the trading-card back instead of a dead "· N" count. Visual/UX only; no Firebase or feature changes; dev channel only.

## Definition of Done

Every archive entry (featured cup + timeline row) leads with its ★ rating + one-word + a tasting pull-quote (degrading gracefully when a bean has no tasting); the trading-card back shows the full tasting per session (stars + aroma/body/finish + notes), not a dead count; the per-row "Show Details" expander and flavor-tag chips are gone; favourite cups stay highlighted on top (editorial featured treatment, lead cup emphasized) with the rest chronological + year-grouped and year headers that pin while their section scrolls; tapping any entry flies the bag into BeanDetailCard via the hero morph; motion (scroll-reveal stagger, parallax bags, spring + haptics) is transform/opacity-only and reduced-motion disables parallax/reveal + the morph; search and every filter still filter both the cups and the timeline and the empty states remain; no Firebase/data/logic/API is touched and no feature is lost; `npx vite build` exits 0; the Archive Playwright harness shows rating + pull-quote per entry, NO Show-Details control and NO row flavor-tag chips, featured cups before a chronological timeline, a working tap→morph, search filtering, reduced-motion with no flight, and zero console errors; verify-inventory and verify-morph still PASS; codex review is clean against the rubric; and Tal signs off on device that the Archive now feels Apple-caliber and the tasting is the visible star.
