## Top 5 issues

1. `HANDBREW_POUROVER_KNOWLEDGE` directly misroutes weak-but-balanced coffee to finer grind and assigns astringency to both finer and coarser adjustments. `BREW_TROUBLESHOOTING_RULES` itself does **not** conflate strength and extraction; the hand-brew block does.
2. “From James Hoffmann” is too broad: the app mixes Atlas-derived summaries, community/Fellow guidance, app-specific adaptations, and named Hoffmann recipes under one attribution.
3. French-press fidelity is materially inconsistent: 75g/L is supported by the Atlas intake, but the recovered published Hoffmann recipe is 60g/L, and the structured 1:13–1:16 range excludes that Hoffmann recipe.
4. The 30-second bloom is defensible as Atlas-derived general advice but not a faithful statement of the recovered Ultimate V60 recipe, which uses roughly 45 seconds and staged pours.
5. All specialty-style freshness windows—especially Apollon’s Gold at 60–90 days—lack style-specific evidence. The extended-rest window conflicts with the supplied generic “first few weeks/within one month” guidance, while `peakStatus.js` adds further unsupported stale thresholds.

The app’s Hoffmann-attributed content is only partially faithful. Its 60g/L V60 ratio, near-boiling water, French-press sequence, medium French-press grind, one-variable-at-a-time principle, and core extraction directions broadly match the supplied Hoffmann/Atlas evidence. However, the blanket attribution overstates provenance: French-press ratio differs across sources, the Ultimate V60 bloom/pour sequence is simplified inaccurately, unsupported bed diagnostics are presented as methodology, and the hand-brew troubleshooting sentence incorrectly merges strength, under-extraction, and astringency. It should be described as mixed Hoffmann-inspired, Atlas-derived, and app-adapted guidance—not a faithful singular representation of Hoffmann’s published methods.
