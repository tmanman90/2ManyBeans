# Koffee Mameya V60 observation (2026-11-27)

Sources: a 4:10 phone video Tal recorded at the omakase counter of Koffee Mameya, Tokyo, plus a photo of the ground coffee, a second 2:28 video and photo (Alo Zero Washed), and the staff's answers to Tal. None of the media is in this repo. Tal gave the dose and water: 12g coffee, 180g water (1:15). Times below are from first water on the customer's brewer (video 1:10.5).

## House recipe (from the staff)

Tal asked the staff after the brews. The house V60 is 12g coffee to 180g water with a pour every 30 seconds. For 20g they pour 100g each time (300g, still 1:15) and the whole brew takes 95 seconds. The app follows these numbers: pours at 0:00, 0:30 and 1:00, dripper off by 1:30 (12g) or 1:35 (20g); other doses interpolate between those two totals.

The videos show the second and third pours starting about 34 to 36 seconds in, not 30. In both videos the barista was pouring other brewers between our pours, so the staff's clock is taken as the recipe and the video gaps as service lag. The pull time (about 1:30) matches the staff's total.

Water temperature is 90C (Tal, after the brew). The app keeps 90C for this method whatever the bean's usual temperature is.

The staff also said they like the V60 for light roasts right now because they brew fast, while other coffees take about 4 minutes on a second brewer (Tal thinks that one was Tres Dragones Natural, Colombia, not confirmed).

Pour path: the camera sat below the rim, so the spout tip is hidden. The path comes from the spout's neck circling in the frames (pour 3 visibly circles, then holds still) and from Tal, who watched it. A first write-up said center only; that was wrong.

## Second video: Alo Zero Washed, Ethiopia (Rose roaster)

A 2:28 video from counter height, so the spout tip and the slurry are visible this time. Same V60 02, same kettle, same barista. Dose was not stated. Times are from first water (video 0:27.5).

| | Pour 1 | Pour 2 | Pour 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start, length | 0:00, about 10 s | 0:33, about 9 s | 1:10, about 12 s |
| Path | center about 2 s, then circles | circles throughout, about 1.1 s per circle (about 7) | circles for about 5 s, then the spout holds still for the last 6 to 7 s |
| Size | the tip swings about 2 cm each side of center, so circles about 3 to 4 cm across | same | same, then still |
| Spout | tip at or just inside the rim, thin stream | same | same |
| Swirl, stir, tap | none | none | none |

- The slurry never rises past about a third of the cone. Dry grounds are still visible at the bottom when pour 1 starts, so there is no separate bloom.
- Between our pours she pours a second brewer to the right (0:16 to 0:26, 0:56 to 1:08, 1:39 to 1:53 after our first water). That is why our pours land about 35 s apart instead of the staff's 30.
- About 1:26 she rests a hand on our dripper and lifts it off at about 1:30, with liquid still on the bed. No swirl before the lift.

Same method as the first video in every respect we can measure.

### The second brewer

A wider, flatter cone than the V60, frosted and translucent with a flat rim, sitting on its own glass server. Its server already held coffee when the video starts, it drips in a thin, steady stream the whole time, and it gets three pours about 40 seconds apart. That fits the staff's "about 4 minutes" brews. The brand and model can't be read from the video, the dose and bean are unknown, and we never see it start, so the app does not add a recipe for it.

## Grind

The clearer (right) bowl shows a uniform medium-coarse grind with flaky particles, like coarse sand to kosher salt. Estimate: about 800 to 950 microns, best guess 850. At the app's micron scales that is roughly Fellow Ode Gen 2 7 to 8 (app picks 7.2 = 836 microns), Fellow Opus 7.7, Comandante 28 clicks, 1Zpresso JX-Pro about 185 clicks, Baratza Virtuoso+ 26. Scale is a guess from the cup size, so treat it as a start point.

Alo Zero Washed (second photo, held over the menu): a much lighter roast color with a lot of papery chaff, and about the same particle size as the first, around 800 microns. The app keeps one grind start point (850 microns) for the method; the difference is within what a photo can show.
