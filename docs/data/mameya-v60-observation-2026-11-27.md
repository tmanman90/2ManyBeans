# Koffee Mameya V60 observation (2026-11-27)

Source: a 4:10 phone video Tal recorded at the omakase counter of Koffee Mameya, Tokyo, plus a photo of the ground coffee. Neither file is in this repo. Tal gave the dose and water: 12g coffee, 180g water (1:15). Times below are from first water on the customer's brewer (video 1:10.5).

## What the video shows

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| prep | Hario V60 02 in clear resin on a glass server, on a scale. Paper already in place. Grounds tipped in from a small steel cup. Black gooseneck kettle (Brewista). |
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Pour 1. Spout low (below the dripper rim), thin slow stream. Held in the center for about the first 2 to 3 seconds, then small circles, about one a second (about 5 to 6 circles) to the end. The water never stands high. Coffee reaches the server by 0:03, so there is no held bloom. |
| 0:10 to 0:35 | Rest. She pours two other guests' brewers in turn. No stir, no swirl, no tapping. |
| 0:35 to 0:46 | Pour 2. Small circles the whole way at the same pace, about one a second (about 8 circles). Same low spout and thin stream. |
| 1:10 to 1:20 | Pour 3. Small circles for about the first 5 seconds (one spiral out and back, about 4 to 5 circles), then the spout holds still in the center for the last 4 to 5 seconds. |
| about 1:28 | A second barista lifts the dripper off the server while liquid is still above the bed. |

The other two brewers got the same treatment and were lifted off about 1:35 after their first water, so the early pull is the house method, not a one-off.

Pour path: the camera sat below the rim, so the spout tip is hidden. The path comes from the spout's neck circling in the frames (pour 3 visibly circles, then holds still) and from Tal, who watched it. A first write-up said center only; that was wrong.

Pour sizes are inferred: three pours of equal length at the same flow, so about 60g each (about 6 g/s). The scale display was not visible.

## Grind

The clearer (right) bowl shows a uniform medium-coarse grind with flaky particles, like coarse sand to kosher salt. Estimate: about 800 to 950 microns, best guess 850. At the app's micron scales that is roughly Fellow Ode Gen 2 7 to 8 (app picks 7.2 = 836 microns), Fellow Opus 7.7, Comandante 28 clicks, 1Zpresso JX-Pro about 185 clicks, Baratza Virtuoso+ 26. Scale is a guess from the cup size, so treat it as a start point.
