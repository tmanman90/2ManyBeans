# AE05 — Watery Kalita

User: I did the Kalita 155 recipe but it felt watered down.
Ruphus: On the hot Kalita, 15g to 250g at Ode 4.2 finished in 3:10, so the thin cup makes sense. I’d go one step finer before changing the dose.

User: Would more dose beat a finer grind?
Ruphus: I’d choose a finer grind first: one step finer keeps the 15g dose and should add extraction and flavor without making the cup heavy.

User: Yes, make that change.
Ruphus: Good call. I’d move the hot Kalita one step finer than Ode 4.2 and keep the 15g dose; that should bring more sweetness and weight.
